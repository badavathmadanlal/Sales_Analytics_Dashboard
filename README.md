## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using Power BI.

The dashboard transforms sales data into visual insights that help analyze:

- Overall income performance
- Total quantity sold
- Product ratings
- City-wise income
- Payment method performance
- Product line contribution
- Gender-wise income
- Product line rating trends
- Branch and city-level sales performance

The dashboard allows users to filter and explore sales performance across different cities, product lines, payment methods and customer segments.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project was built to answer the following business questions:

### OBJECTIVE 01

Measure overall business performance using total income and quantity sold.

### OBJECTIVE 02

Compare sales performance across different cities.

### OBJECTIVE 03

Analyze income generated through different payment methods.

### OBJECTIVE 04

Identify product lines contributing the highest share of total income.

### OBJECTIVE 05

Compare sales performance across branches.

### OBJECTIVE 06

Analyze income distribution by gender.

### OBJECTIVE 07

Compare average customer ratings across different product lines.

# 📈 Dashboard Analysis

## 🏙️ Total Income by City

The dashboard compares total income across:

- Mumbai
- Hyderabad
- Delhi

Mumbai shows the highest total income among the displayed cities.

---

## 💳 Total Income by Payment and City

Sales are analyzed across different payment methods:

- Cash
- E-wallet
- Credit Card

The comparison is further segmented by city to understand customer payment preferences.

---

## 📦 Total Income by Product Line

The dashboard visualizes income contribution across multiple product lines, including:

- Food and Beverages
- Electronic Accessories
- Sports and Travel
- Fashion Accessories
- Home and Lifestyle
- Health and Beauty

This analysis helps identify which product categories contribute the most to overall income.

---

## 👥 Total Income by Gender

The dashboard compares income generated across customer gender segments.

This provides an additional perspective for understanding sales distribution and customer purchasing patterns.

---

## 📊 Income by City and Branch

Sales performance is compared across:

- Cities
- Branches

This allows the business to identify stronger and weaker sales locations.

---

## ⭐ Average Rating by Product Line

Customer ratings are analyzed across different product lines.

The dashboard helps compare customer satisfaction levels between product categories.
